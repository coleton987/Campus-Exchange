About Campus Exchange

Your trusted, secure marketplace exclusively for Bob Jones University students. Buy, sell, and connect with your campus community safely and easily.

Our Mission

"To create a safe, trusted marketplace where BJU students can easily buy and sell items within their campus community, fostering connections and helping students save money while maintaining the highest standards of security and integrity."

What We Do

Campus Exchange is a private, university-exclusive marketplace designed specifically for Bob Jones University students. Unlike public platforms like Facebook Marketplace or Craigslist, we provide a secure environment where you know exactly who you're dealing with - fellow BJU students.

Whether you're looking to sell textbooks after a semester, find affordable dorm furniture, or trade electronics, Campus Exchange makes it simple and safe to connect with buyers and sellers in your campus community.

Your Safety Matters

We prioritize student safety above all else. All users must verify their BJU email address, and we encourage all meetings to take place in public, well-lit areas on campus. Our community guidelines ensure respectful interactions, and we have reporting mechanisms for any inappropriate behavior.

Safety Tips:

  • Always meet in public campus locations
  • Bring a friend if you feel more comfortable
  • Inspect items carefully before purchasing
  • Trust your instincts - if something feels off, don't proceed
  • Report any suspicious or inappropriate behavior immediately
