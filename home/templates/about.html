About Campus Exchange

Your trusted campus marketplace for Bob Jones University students

Our Mission

Campus Exchange provides a secure, private marketplace exclusively for Bob Jones University students. We're here to help you buy and sell textbooks, electronics, furniture, and other items safely within your campus community.

How It Works

1. Sign Up

Create your account using your @bju.edu email address. This ensures only BJU students can access the marketplace.

2. List Your Items

Upload photos and descriptions of items you want to sell. Set your price and add any relevant details.

3. Browse & Search

Find what you need using our search and filter options. Browse by category, price range, or keywords.

4. Connect

Message sellers directly through our secure in-app messaging system to ask questions and arrange meetups.

5. Meet & Exchange

Meet in person on campus to complete your transaction safely. Cash or item exchanges only - no online payments needed.
